1. OVERALL NATIONAL UNEMPLOYMENT RATE:
   Across all observations from May 2019 to June 2020, the national average unemployment rate stood at 11.79%, with a median of 8.35%.

2. HIGHEST AND LOWEST RECORDED SINGLE RATES:
   - Peak single observation: 76.74% in Puducherry (Urban) during April 2020.
   - Minimum single observation: 0.00% in Puducherry during May 2019.

3. PEAK MONTHLY NATIONAL SHOCK (COVID-19 LOCKDOWN):
   - The national average unemployment rate spiked to its highest monthly level in May 2020 at 24.88%.
   - In contrast, the lowest monthly national average was recorded in May 2019 at 8.87%.

4. COVID-19 IMPACT (PRE-COVID VS COVID-19 PERIOD):
   - Pre-COVID Average (May 2019 - Feb 2020): 9.51%
   - COVID-19 Period Average (Mar 2020 - Jun 2020): 17.77%
   - Absolute Increase: +8.26 percentage points
   - Relative Surge: +86.91% increase in average unemployment rate.

5. URBAN VS RURAL DISPARITY:
   - Urban areas suffered higher average unemployment at 13.17%, compared to 10.32% in Rural areas.
   - Urban economies experienced sharper lockdown vulnerability due to service-sector closures and dense labor markets.

6. REGIONAL AND STATE-LEVEL DISPARITIES:
   - Highest average unemployment rate by state: Tripura with an average of 28.35%.
   - Lowest average unemployment rate by state: Meghalaya with an average of 4.80%.
   - Demonstrates marked regional inequality in labor market resilience.

7. POLICY IMPLICATIONS & OBSERVATIONAL LIMITATIONS:
   - Urban labor markets require targeted emergency social safety nets and wage subsidy schemes during macroeconomic disruptions.
   - Rural agricultural activity provided partial absorption capacity during lockdowns, but requires continuous formalization.
   - Limitation: These observational data reflect sample survey estimates; changes during 2020 coincide with COVID-19
     containment measures but do not isolate other simultaneous supply-chain and global macroeconomic shifts.
